mon1 is UNSTABLE: >2x scatter of mon4 - bucketing artifact likely


Yes, it's due to bucketing—but not a selection failure, a design flaw.
The bucketing algorithm works correctly: mon1 selected options ARE closer to the delta target (-0.0625) than mon4 are to theirs (-0.75). The algorithm picked well.
The problem is the bucketing criterion itself:
At ATM (delta ≈ -0.0625), the delta curve is flat. Small moneyness changes = small delta changes. So the closeness metric can't distinguish between options with quite different moneyness, as long as they have similar delta.
Result: moneyness scatter in mon1 is 2.23x higher than mon4, even though selection is working correctly.
This is a bucketing design issue, not a data quality issue:

Delta-based bucketing works well for deep OTM (delta curve steep) → mon4 is stable
Delta-based bucketing fails at ATM (delta curve flat) → mon1 is inherently unstable

Your tau(q) curves are unstable across maturity because the buckets contain genuinely heterogeneous contracts (different moneyness, similar delta). This is exactly what you observed in the panels.
Bottom line: Don't blame the data or the algorithm. Blame the bucketing design. If you want stable mon1 buckets, you need a bucketing criterion that works at ATM—probably moneyness-based rather than delta-based for that region.
